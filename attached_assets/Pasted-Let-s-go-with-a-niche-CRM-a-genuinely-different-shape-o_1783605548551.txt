Let's go with a niche CRM — a genuinely different shape of business than the guitar app (B2B, subscription, not AI-dependent, less consumer marketing needed).

Concept: CRM for Veterinary Clinics

Why this one: Generic CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) are overkill and don't understand vet-specific workflows — patient (pet) history tied to owner, recall reminders for vaccines, multi-pet households, emergency triage notes. Vet clinics are underserved because they're not a "sexy" market big CRM companies chase, but there are ~30,000+ independent vet clinics in the US alone, most running on paper, spreadsheets, or clunky legacy software (Cornerstone, AVImark) that hasn't been redesigned in a decade.

Core loop (MVP, nothing else):





Front desk creates a client (owner) + patient (pet) record



Log a visit: reason, notes, weight, meds prescribed



System auto-schedules the next recall (e.g., "rabies due in 12 months") and sends the clinic a reminder



Clinic sends the client an automated text/email reminder when it's due



Simple dashboard: today's appointments, overdue recalls, recent visits

What to explicitly leave OUT of v1:





No billing/invoicing integration (huge scope creep — Stripe integration alone can eat a month)



No inventory management (medication stock tracking)



No multi-location support



No custom form builder — hardcode the fields vets actually need

Tech shape:





Web app (desktop-first — front desk staff use a computer, not a phone)



Simple database: owners, pets, visits, recall rules



Twilio or similar for SMS reminders



Basic role-based login (front desk vs. vet vs. admin)

Monetization: $50–100/month per clinic (much higher willingness to pay than consumer apps — this saves staff time and reduces missed recalls, which directly ties to revenue for the clinic).

Validation before building:





Call or visit 10 independent vet clinics in your area. Ask what they currently use and what annoys them about it (missed recalls, clunky old software, no texting reminders)



Offer to build a very basic version for one clinic for free or cheap in exchange for being your design partner



If they say yes and actually use it daily for a few weeks, you have a real MVP with a paying reference customer waiting

Timeline if validated: 3–4 weeks for a working version handling the core loop above — this is straightforward CRUD + a scheduling/reminder engine, no AI or hard technical risk involved.
